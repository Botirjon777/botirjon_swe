Privacy policy

"Postiz Publishing" by Botirjon (@botirjon_swe). Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What the app is

Postiz Publishing is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner, Botirjon, to publish videos to his own social media accounts (YouTube channel BI Code, Instagram @botirjon_swe, Telegram channel Code With Botirjon) and to read and answer the comments and messages on those accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

  • The access the account owner grants when signing in with Google, Meta or Telegram, stored on the owner's computer only.
  • From YouTube (YouTube Data API): the owner's channel, its videos, and the public comments on them, to show them in the owner's inbox and to post the owner's replies and uploads.
  • From Instagram and Facebook: the owner's posts, the comments on them and the messages sent to the owner's account, to show them in the owner's inbox and to send the owner's replies.
  • Public information that commenters show on these platforms (their username and comment text).

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • It does not use the data for advertising or profiling.
  • It does not access any account other than the owner's own.

Replies may be drafted with the help of an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) from the comment text; only the text needed for the reply is used.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept on the owner's computer only as long as it is needed to publish and to answer comments. Access can be revoked at any time in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions (and in Meta's and Telegram's settings), after which the app can no longer read or post anything.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Botirjon777/botirjon_swe or send a message to @botirjon_swe on Instagram.
